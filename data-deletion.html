User Data Deletion Instructions

How to revoke Facebook access and request deletion of information associated with OpenNews AI MVP.

Effective and last updated: 2 October 2026

Option 1: Remove the app from Facebook

You can revoke the app’s Facebook access directly from your Facebook account. Facebook menu names may vary slightly by device.

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business integrations or Apps and websites.
  3. Find OpenNews AI MVP.
  4. Select it and choose Remove, then confirm.

Removing the integration stops future API access. You should also delete the related Page access token from your local OpenNews AI .env file if you operate the MVP yourself.

Option 2: Email a deletion request

Send an email to mahmudulhasanrobin.official@gmail.com with the subject:

OpenNews AI Data Deletion Request

Include only the following information so the request can be identified:

  • Your Facebook Page name.
  • Your Facebook Page ID, if available.
  • The email address you want us to use for confirmation.
Never email your password, one-time password, App Secret, User Token, or Page Access Token.

What will be deleted

After a valid request is verified, we will delete information controlled by the MVP that is associated with the connection, including where applicable:

  • Saved Facebook Page identifiers and locally stored access tokens.
  • Facebook post identifiers and publishing-history records.
  • Associated local configuration and diagnostic records that are not required for security or legal compliance.

What may require a separate action

Deleting information from OpenNews AI does not automatically delete posts that are already public on Facebook or copies of website content. A Page administrator can delete those posts from the Facebook Page and remove published website files separately.

Processing time

We normally acknowledge deletion requests within 7 days and complete valid requests within 30 days. We may retain limited records when required by law, fraud prevention, or security obligations.

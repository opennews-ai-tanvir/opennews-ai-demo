OpenNews AI MVP

Privacy Policy

How the OpenNews AI student demonstration project handles information when connecting to a Facebook Page and preparing news content.

Effective: 2 October 2026Last updated: 2 October 2026
Plain-language summary: OpenNews AI does not sell personal information. The current MVP connects only to a Page selected by its administrator, creates draft news copy, requires human approval, and publishes only when an authorized user presses the publish button.

1. About this project

OpenNews AI MVP (“OpenNews AI”, “we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a student demonstration project for collecting news from publicly available sources, generating draft Bengali copy with artificial intelligence, and publishing approved content to a website or an authorized Facebook Page. It is currently operated as a limited MVP and is not yet offered as a public multi-customer service.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features used, OpenNews AI may process:

  • Facebook Page identifiers, Page name, and the permissions approved by the Page administrator.
  • Facebook access tokens required to connect to and publish on the selected Page.
  • News headlines, summaries, captions, source names, source links, categories, approval status, and Facebook post identifiers.
  • Basic technical information and error messages needed to diagnose failures and prevent duplicate publishing.

OpenNews AI does not request Facebook passwords or one-time passwords and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

3. How we use information

We use the information only to:

  • Connect to the Facebook Page selected by an authorized administrator.
  • Prepare, review, approve, and publish news posts.
  • Record successful post identifiers and prevent accidental duplicate publishing.
  • Maintain security, diagnose errors, and improve the MVP.

4. AI-assisted processing and human approval

Publicly available news text may be sent to an AI service, such as Google Gemini, to generate draft headlines, summaries, or captions. AI output is treated as a draft. OpenNews AI requires a human reviewer to verify and approve content before publication.

5. Storage and security

During the MVP stage, configuration secrets and publishing records are stored locally on the operator’s controlled Windows computer using an environment file and a local SQLite database. Access tokens are not intentionally displayed in the app, included in public website files, or uploaded to the public GitHub repository. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing and third-party services

Information is shared only as necessary to operate the requested features, including with Meta/Facebook for Page authorization and publishing, and with Google Gemini for AI-assisted drafting. Public news sources are linked and credited. We do not sell or rent personal information.

7. Retention

Page connection details and publishing records are retained only while needed for the MVP, security, duplicate prevention, or legal obligations. Access can be revoked and deletion can be requested at any time. Valid deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days, unless retention is required by law or for security.

8. Your choices and data deletion

A Page administrator may stop using the app, revoke Facebook permissions from Facebook settings, and request deletion of information associated with the connection. Detailed instructions are provided on the OpenNews AI Data Deletion Instructions page.

9. Children’s privacy

OpenNews AI is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the project develops. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
